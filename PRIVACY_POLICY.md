# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: September 26, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Flash (“Flash,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, discloses, and otherwise processes information when you use the Flash language-learning application and related services.

## 1. About Flash

Flash is a language-learning application providing interactive learning sessions, adaptive learning functionality, spaced review, progress tracking, progression systems, and related language-learning features.

Flash currently supports English and Spanish learning experiences.

## 2. Information We Process

The information Flash processes depends on how you use the App and which features are enabled.

### 2.1 Account Information

If you create a registered account, Flash may process:

- User ID
- Email address
- Display name
- Avatar or profile information
- Authentication provider
- Account settings
- Account status

Flash may also allow guest use without a registered account.

## 3. Authentication

Flash uses authentication services including Firebase Authentication.

Authentication methods may include:

- Email and password
- Google Sign-In
- Guest use
- Other methods made available in future versions

Where you use a third-party authentication provider, that provider may process information according to its own privacy practices.

## 4. Learning and Progress Data

Flash processes learning information in order to provide and personalize the learning experience.

This may include:

- Selected learning language

- Vocabulary progress
- Questions presented
- Answers submitted
- Correct and incorrect responses
- Accuracy
- Session history
- Review scheduling
- Spaced-repetition state
- Mastery or learning progress
- Daily objectives
- Adaptive-learning state
- Custom vocabulary collections
- Saved content
- Activity and timing information

Flash uses this information to provide learning sessions, track progress, schedule review, and adapt aspects of the learning experience.
