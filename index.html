Why Flash

Traditional study methods — word lists, flashcard apps, grammar drills — demand too much time and produce too little engagement. Most learners stop before real retention happens.

⚡

Lower friction

Sessions are short and immediate. No setup, no long lessons to load. You practice in minutes, not hours.

🔁

Built-in repetition

Adaptive review targets the words you need most. Progress does not rely on discipline alone — the system guides the loop.

🎯

Outcome-focused

Flash is built around vocabulary acquisition and sentence pattern recognition — the two things that move real language ability forward.

Account Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Flash app on your device
  2. Navigate to Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom to find Danger Zone
  4. Tap Delete Account
  5. Confirm the deletion when prompted

⚠️ Note: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What Data Is Deleted

  • Firebase Authentication account — Your login credentials and authentication data
  • User profile information — Name, email, profile settings
  • Gameplay statistics — XP, levels, streaks, match history
  • Vocabulary progress — Word unlock state, learning progress
  • Collections and achievements — Custom word lists, badges
  • App preferences — Settings, notification preferences
  • Local caches — All app data stored on your device

What Data May Be Retained

  • Purchase records — Transaction history may be retained for up to 7 years as required by Google Play for legal, tax, and financial compliance
  • Aggregated analytics — Anonymous, non-identifiable usage data may be retained for product improvement
  • Support logs — If you contacted support, tickets may be retained for quality assurance purposes

Retained data contains no personally identifiable information and cannot be linked back to you.
